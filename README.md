# Blinkit Business Analytics | Excel

A multi-sheet Excel project analyzing a retail delivery business across orders, customers, products, inventory, delivery performance, marketing, and customer feedback.

## Workbook contents
- Analysis
- Customer feedback and customer records
- Delivery performance
- Inventory
- Marketing performance
- Order items and orders
- Products

## Skills demonstrated
- Business data organization
- Retail operations analysis
- Excel-based reporting

## Data privacy
The workbook contains customer-related sample records. Its email fields use example.com, example.net, and example.org domains, and its phone fields use repeated placeholder-style values.

## Open the project
Download `Excel Project Dipl.xlsx` and open it in Microsoft Excel.
